SEO

Cutting 61% of wasted impressions without losing a click

Channel
Organic search
Client
B2B SaaS / services
Window
Sep 2025 – Jan 2026 vs. prior period

Situation

Not every SEO win looks like more traffic. This account ranked for a wide spread of loosely relevant terms, racking up impressions that rarely converted into clicks. Average position sat around 62, deep on page six or beyond.

What I did

  • Audited the keyword set for relevance rather than raw volume
  • Deliberately tightened targeting toward terms that matched buyer intent, accepting that total impressions would fall
  • Rebuilt on-page content around the narrower, higher-intent term set

Results, verified

62.1 → 31.1
Average position, roughly halved
0.2% → 0.7%
Average CTR, nearly 4×
557K → 217K
Impressions, cut by design
1.32K → 1.47K
Clicks, held and ticked up
"

Anyone can chase impression volume. Choosing to shrink it on purpose, and proving clicks didn't suffer, is a judgment call that separates a manager from an executor.
